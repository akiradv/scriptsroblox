FAQ

answers to the most common questions.

what is scripts.dev?
a library of handcrafted, open-source roblox scripts. every script is built, tested and maintained by the community, with the source fully public on github.
how does the universal loader work?
the loader automatically detects which game you're playing and loads the appropriate script. just copy the loadstring, paste it in your executor, and run it inside any supported game.
are the scripts free?
yes. 100% free and open-source. no keys, no paid tiers, no ad-links, no subscriptions. if someone is selling our scripts, it's a scam — report it on our discord.
which executors are supported?
any executor that supports loadstring and standard lua. we test on the most popular ones. if a script fails on your executor, try another before reporting a bug.
will I get banned?
no third-party software can guarantee that. our scripts are built to be lightweight and run locally, but using them is at your own risk and may violate roblox's terms. if you're unsure, use an alt account.
why is my script not working?
roblox or the game itself may have updated and broken something. check the script's status on the site or the #status channel on discord. we fix issues as soon as they're reported.
how do I request a new game?
join the discord and use !suggest or the suggestions channel. the roadmap is driven by community votes.
how do I report a bug?
open a ticket on discord or open an issue on github. include the script name, your executor and what happened.
do my settings get saved?
yes. configs are stored locally on your device by your executor. nothing is uploaded to us — we never see your settings.
